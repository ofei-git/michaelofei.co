Editorial work and media projects.

I lead editorial projects and make content myself. Here’s what I’ve done with Backlinko, The Minimalist Vegan and Heartful Table.

Backlinko

I joined in December 2023 to lead the editorial program Brian Dean started.

Brian founded Backlinko in 2012 and became known for practical SEO guides and ideas such as the Skyscraper Technique. Semrush acquired the site in 2022.

As lead editor, I built the team and the processes behind the written work. That included more than 30 editorial guidelines, templates and process documents for briefs, reviews, expert input, hiring and production. I also edited and wrote major pieces myself.

I’m still involved. I look after the site and newsletter, guide the remaining written work and make routine publishing decisions. I also script and present videos for Backlinko.

My role

I joined as lead editor, built the editorial team and processes, set the quality bar and wrote flagship pieces. Today I look after the publication and script and present videos for the channel.

The results reflect the work of many people across Backlinko and Semrush.

The Minimalist Vegan

An independent publication Maša and I launched in March 2015 and sold in January 2023.

My wife, Maša, and I started The Minimalist Vegan while we were becoming vegan and simplifying our lives. We wrote about the choices we were making, the questions we had and what we learned along the way.

Over time, we published long-form articles, wrote a book, recorded more than 70 podcast episodes and built a community around the site. I led the SEO strategy as The Minimalist Vegan attracted 1.5 million website visitors and more than 2 million pageviews in its peak year, 2021.

My role

I led the editorial and SEO strategy, wrote and published articles, produced the podcast and ran the systems behind the site.

We sold The Minimalist Vegan in January 2023. The site has changed since then, so the book, podcast and archived pages are a better record of what we built. See the acquisition context.

Heartful Table

Producing practical cooking videos for Maša’s plant-based recipe site.

Heartful Table is the plant-based recipe site Maša runs. I led its content and SEO strategy and produced 27 videos for its YouTube channel.

I researched what people wanted to cook before planning each video. I planned the format, directed the shoot, filmed and edited, chose the music and packaged the finished video for YouTube. Maša developed the recipes, styled the kitchen and food and appeared on camera.

My role

I researched demand, planned and directed the videos, filmed and edited them, chose the music and packaged each release for YouTube.

These videos were made several years ago. Heartful Table remains active under Maša’s lead.
