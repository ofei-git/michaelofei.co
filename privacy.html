Privacy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a personal website. Here is how information is handled when you visit or get in touch.

Analytics

This site uses Clicky for basic analytics, including page views, referrers and general geographic region. Clicky's privacy documentation explains its visitor privacy settings and how it processes analytics data.

Contact enquiries

When you use the contact form, your name, email address, optional company name, enquiry type and message are processed by Formspree, stored in its submissions inbox and emailed to me. I use this information to respond to your enquiry. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

Previous newsletter subscriptions

This site no longer accepts newsletter signups. If you previously subscribed, your email address may still be stored by Buttondown. You can contact me to request removal. Removing the signup forms does not delete existing subscriptions.

Other services

This site is static HTML hosted on GitHub Pages. I also use the LinkedIn API to publish to my personal LinkedIn profile. No LinkedIn user data is collected, stored or processed through this site; that integration is used to publish my own content.

Questions or deletion requests

Email michael@ofei.co with questions about this policy or to request deletion of an enquiry or previous newsletter subscription.
